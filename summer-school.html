01

Who it’s for

The summer school takes place every August and welcomes students who are about to start their BSc programmes at TU Delft in September. It revisits secondary-school mathematics through lectures, discussion and problem solving, with opportunities to meet fellow students before the academic year begins.

02

Programme

The syllabus follows Dutch Wiskunde B. The five-day 2026 edition covered the following topics:

August 2026Topics
Thursday 6Polynomial and rational functions
Friday 7Trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions
Monday 10Differentiation
Tuesday 11Integration
Wednesday 12Further calculus, counting and vectors

Each day combined lectures with exercise classes. Teaching assistants helped with questions about both the exercises and the lecture material.

03

My role

I am an organizer and lecturer for the TU Delft Mathematics Summer School, which has run from 2023 onwards. The aim is to help students refresh their Wiskunde B mathematics before they start their BSc programmes.
